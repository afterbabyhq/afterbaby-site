Your body

A thirty-second check-in

Mood, pain, bleeding, sleep — logged with one hand, in the dark, without judgment. Gentle week-by-week guidance and the warning signs that should never be ignored.

Your mind

Someone finally asks

The world's most trusted postpartum wellbeing screen, taken quietly on your own terms — with trusted support lines one tap away, any hour.

Your voice

Walk in with data

Everything you log becomes a visit brief your provider can act on. Walk into your checkup heard, not hurried.

Your village

Mothers, nearby

Find classes, storytimes, playgroups, and family events near you today — and soon, low-pressure plans with moms at your stage: a walk, a coffee, a both-awake-anyway.

Private by design

Your data never leaves your phone.

No account. No cloud. No tracking. Nothing sold — ever. AfterBaby stores everything on your own device, because the most personal season of your life deserves the most personal privacy there is.

Support

We answer, gently and quickly.

Questions, feedback, or help with the app: hello@afterbaby.health
